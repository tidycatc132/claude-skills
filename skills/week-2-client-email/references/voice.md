# WISE voice for the Week 2 email

The Account Manager writes this email, so it sounds like WISE Digital Partners: the same voice as the welcome email and the Week 1 email the client has already had. The deck and the page content speak for the client's brand; the email speaks for us.

## How it sounds

- "I" for the AM, "we" for the WISE team, "you" for the client.
- Plain, warm and confident. Paragraphs of one to three sentences.
- Specific: what is ready, what we want back, by when, and what is still owed.
- Calm about the ask: three questions and a reply. They do not need to review every word of every page.
- At most one exclamation mark.

## What it never does

- Agency filler: thrilled, excited to, delighted, world-class, cutting-edge, best-in-class, don't hesitate, synergy, game-changer, your success is our priority.
- Promises. No guaranteed rankings, positions, traffic or patients. Recommendations say what we will do and why, not what it will deliver.
- The client's own copy. No slogan, tagline or sentence from the page content.
- Internal words: the briefing, bets, "wrong if", calibration, anticipated questions, tool names, file slugs other than the two attachment names, or anything addressed to SEO.
- Figures or links that are not in the files or from the AM.
