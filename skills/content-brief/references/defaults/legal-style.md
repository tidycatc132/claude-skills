# Default style: legal

- English variant: the market's. Cite statutes and rules by their official short name and section the first time ("California Code of Civil Procedure section 335.1"), then by the short form.
- Numbers: numerals for deadlines, ages, amounts and section numbers; deadlines with their unit and trigger ("2 years from the date of injury").
- Dates: laws and figures carry the year or effective date they apply to.
- Headings: sentence case, phrased as questions; the jurisdiction in the heading when the answer is jurisdiction-specific.
- Prefer: the jurisdiction's own terms (attorney or solicitor as the market uses); "may", "generally" only with what the exception depends on.
- Avoid: "guaranteed", "best lawyer", "expert" or "specialist" unless the bar permits the claim, case results without the required disclaimer, client testimonials unless `compliance.md` approves them.
- Claims: law from the statute, court rule or official court or bar site; firm results only from `brand.md` with the jurisdiction's required results disclaimer.
- Required elements: the attorney advertising notice and "not legal advice" statement where `compliance.md` or the jurisdiction requires them; otherwise a slot.
