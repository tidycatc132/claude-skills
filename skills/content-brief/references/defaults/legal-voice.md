# Default voice: legal

- Speaker: the firm, first person plural, with a named attorney (bar admission and state) as author or reviewer of every page.
- Reading level: grade 8 to 10. Define every legal term the first time it appears and name the jurisdiction it applies to.
- Do explain the process, the deadlines and the options in order; don't give advice on the reader's specific case.
- Do state when the answer depends on the jurisdiction or the facts, and what it depends on; don't generalize one state's law to every reader.
- Do keep a calm, respectful tone for readers who may be in distress; don't dramatize, threaten or promise outcomes ("we will win your case").
- Do say when to talk to a lawyer and what to bring; don't imply an attorney-client relationship from reading the page.
- Call to action: a consultation or contact step, with what happens next, after the explanation.
