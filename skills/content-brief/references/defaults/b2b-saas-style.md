# Default style: B2B, SaaS and professional services

- English variant: the market's. Product and feature names exactly as the client writes them, including capitalization.
- Numbers: numerals for metrics, prices, counts and durations; percentages with "%"; prices with the billing unit ("$49 per user per month").
- Dates: pricing, benchmarks and feature claims carry the date they were true.
- Headings: sentence case, phrased as questions.
- Prefer: the specific job the reader does ("reconcile invoices", "onboard a new hire"), standard industry terms with their abbreviation defined once.
- Avoid: "best-in-class", "seamless", "leverage", "synergy", "cutting-edge", "AI-powered" without saying what the AI does, unverifiable superlatives.
- Claims: product capabilities and prices from `brand.md` or the client's live pricing page; benchmarks and market statistics from the original research with its sample and date; customer results only when `brand.md` names the customer and approves the figure.
- Comparisons: competitor facts from the competitor's own current documentation or pricing page, dated.
