# Default voice: home services

- Speaker: the business, in the first person plural ("we"), with the named author's field experience in the first person where the brief gives it.
- Reading level: grade 7 to 9. Short sentences; one idea per sentence.
- Do explain what is happening inside the equipment in plain words ("scale coats the heat exchanger"); don't use trade jargon without a one-line definition.
- Do give the homeowner a clear do-it-yourself boundary ("you can flush the unit; gas line work needs a licensed plumber"); don't encourage unsafe DIY on gas, electrical or structural work.
- Do sound like a calm technician at the door: direct, practical, no alarm; don't use scare tactics ("disaster", "nightmare") or urgency that is not real.
- Do state prices and timings as ranges or "starting at" with what changes them; don't promise exact prices or same-day service unless `brand.md` says so.
- Do admit when the answer depends on the home, then say what it depends on; don't hedge every sentence.
- Call to action: one specific next step (book, call, get a quote), with the route, after the reader has their answer; never before the first answer.
