# Default style: home services

- English variant: the market's (en-us: American spelling). Units: the market's (en-us: gallons, °F, feet).
- Numbers: numerals for all measurements, prices, ages and counts above nine; "$3,850", "20 years", "3 to 8 ppm". Ranges with "to", not a hyphen, in prose.
- Dates: "October 2026" in prose; ISO in tables.
- Headings: sentence case, phrased as questions.
- Prefer: the specific equipment name ("tankless water heater", "heat pump"), "licensed plumber/electrician" with the license type, "service area" towns by name.
- Avoid: "state-of-the-art", "top-notch", "best in town", "affordable" without a price, "guaranteed" unless `brand.md` states the guarantee, brand names the client does not service.
- Claims: lifespans, efficiency and savings come from a manufacturer, government or standards source in the Evidence table; the client's own job numbers are labeled as the client's records; license numbers only as written in `brand.md`.
- Safety: any step involving gas, electricity, refrigerant or structural work carries a **Safety:** line and says when to call a licensed professional.
