# Default voice: financial services

- Speaker: the institution, in the first person plural ("we"), with a named, credentialed reviewer for any page that explains a product, rate or risk.
- Reading level: grade 8 to 10. Define every financial term the first time it appears ("loan-to-value ratio (LTV), the share of your home's value you owe").
- Do explain how the product works and what it costs before why to choose it; don't lead with the sales pitch.
- Do state risks plainly and early (variable rates, collateral, fees); don't bury them or soften them ("a small chance").
- Do write for a first-time borrower or saver without talking down; don't assume they know the jargon.
- Do say what depends on the member's situation and point to a person or tool for their numbers; don't give individual advice ("you should borrow").
- Do keep a calm, neutral, factual tone; don't use urgency, scarcity or promises ("lock in before it's too late", "guaranteed approval").
- Call to action: one next step (compare rates, talk to a loan officer, apply), after the explanation and the risks.
