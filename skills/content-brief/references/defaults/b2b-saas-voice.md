# Default voice: B2B, SaaS and professional services

- Speaker: the company, first person plural, with a named practitioner (role and years) as author for how-to and opinion pages.
- Reading level: grade 9 to 11 for a professional reader; define product-category jargon once.
- Do lead with the outcome the reader's team gets and the effort it takes; don't lead with features.
- Do show how the thing works with a concrete, real workflow from the client's product or customers; don't invent customer stories or metrics.
- Do be direct and confident, with specifics; don't use hype ("revolutionary", "game-changing") or filler ("in today's fast-paced world").
- Do name competitors and alternatives fairly when comparing, with sourced facts; don't disparage.
- Call to action: one next step matched to the funnel (guide or template at TOFU, demo or trial at BOFU), after the answer.
