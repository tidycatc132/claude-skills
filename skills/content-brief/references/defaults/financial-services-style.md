# Default style: financial services

- English variant: the market's. Currency: the market's symbol before the number ("$400"); percentages as numerals with "%" ("7.25%"), "APR" spelled out once as annual percentage rate (APR).
- Numbers: numerals for amounts, rates, terms and ages; terms in years or months ("10-year draw period" only when sourced).
- Dates: every rate, fee or limit carries the date it applies to.
- Headings: sentence case, phrased as questions.
- Prefer: the regulator's and the institution's own product names (home equity line of credit (HELOC), certificate rather than CD if the client says so); "may", "can" for features that depend on approval.
- Avoid: "free money", "guaranteed approval", "best rates" without a dated comparison, "risk-free", "no credit check" unless true and approved, any specific rate, payment or term not in the Evidence table or `compliance.md`.
- Claims: product terms (rates, limits, draw and repayment periods, fees) only from the client's approved sources; general facts about how a product works from a regulator (for example the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau) or the Federal Reserve. A specific rate or payment can trigger advertising disclosure rules: such a figure is a client-input slot unless `compliance.md` approves it.
- Required elements: the client's required disclosures and lender statements from `compliance.md`, placed as it says; otherwise a slot for them.
