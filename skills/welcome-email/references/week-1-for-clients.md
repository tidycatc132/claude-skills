# What the client is told about Week 1

Use these facts, in the client's terms, for the "what happens next" part of the email. Keep it to three or four short sentences of prose, not a list: the only numbered list in the email is the open questions, and `check_email.py` treats every numbered line as one. Nothing here is a date.

1. **The intake form (now).** About 15 minutes. It collects what the conversations so far can't give us:
   - access to their platforms (website hosting, domain, Google Analytics and Search Console, Google Business Profile), granted by inviting WISE as a user, never by sending passwords;
   - business details: address, phone, hours as they should appear on the site;
   - brand files: logo, colors, fonts, photos, and any brand or style preferences not settled yet;
   - the few open questions from the email, answered in the form's last section.
   Anything they don't have handy can wait for the Kickoff Call.
2. **The Kickoff Call.** Booked through the link in the email (or, with no link, by replying with a few times that suit them). It covers what the form left open and the goals for the new site.
3. **After the call.** WISE researches their market and plans the site's pages, then writes the homepage copy.
4. **The first thing they review.** 2 to 3 homepage design options, sent with the homepage copy, for their feedback.

Do not mention Synup, listings, the Growth Blueprint, ClickUp, Claude, or internal steps such as editorial review.
