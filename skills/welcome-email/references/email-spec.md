# Welcome email file

Path: `welcome-email/<slug>/<slug>-welcome-email.md`, where `<slug>` is the prefix of the kickoff files (`kickoff/<slug>/<slug>-gap-questions.md`).

```
Client: <Client Name>
Subject: <subject line>

Hi <first name>,

<body>

<AM full name>
Account Manager, WISE Digital Partners
```

- Line 1 is `Client: <Client Name>`, copied from the gap questions file. Line 2 is `Subject: ` and a subject of 70 characters or fewer that names the client or the project. Line 3 is blank. The rest is the email exactly as it will be pasted, in plain text (no markdown headings, bold or bullets with `*`).
- Greeting: the owner or decision-maker the context file names, even when someone else handles day-to-day contact (that person can be mentioned in the body). Use their first name, or `Dr. <last name>` when the context gives them the title Dr. If the context names no owner or decision-maker, use "Hi <Client Name> team,".
- Body order: who the AM is and one specific line showing we listened; the intake form link; the open questions; the Kickoff Call; what happens after the call; sign-off.
- Open questions: each on its own line as `1. <question>`, numbered from 1 in the order of the gap questions file, copied word for word. A question is open when its `Answer:` line is empty. Questions the AM answered or deleted are left out, and the remaining ones are renumbered. Introduce them as a few things we couldn't answer from our conversations, to answer in the form's last section. With no open questions, write no list and no question section.
- Links: the intake form link exactly as given; the Kickoff Call booking link exactly as given, or, with none, ask them to reply with a few times that suit them. No other links.
- Length: at most 250 words outside the numbered questions.
- Sign-off: the AM's full name, then `Account Manager, WISE Digital Partners`.
