# Output guide

## Key Observations (in the sheet, prospect-facing)

Write 3 to 5 observations. Each one:

- Is one or two plain sentences a business owner understands without SEO knowledge.
- Ties a finding from the audit to something the prospect said on the call, where possible ("You mentioned most calls come from Round Rock, but the site has no page for Round Rock and does not appear on page one there.").
- States the problem and its effect on leads or revenue, not the fix. The fix is the sales conversation.
- Uses a number from the audit when one exists.

Order them by business impact, biggest first.

Never include: budget, objections, competitor names the prospect mentioned, internal opinions, guesses about their staff, or anything said off-the-record on the call.

## Sales notes (internal file)

Fill [../assets/sales-notes-template.md](../assets/sales-notes-template.md) and save it as `<slug>-sales-notes.md`. Be candid and short. Quote the prospect for every claim about what they said: copy the words exactly as they appear in the transcript cell (a whole sentence or a shorter run of it, never a paraphrase), in quotation marks, followed by the timestamp or row number, for example `"We don't show up in Round Rock at all." (00:02:30)`.
