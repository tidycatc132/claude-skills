# Video SERP / YouTube Outliers

Use this when the SERP has a video pack or the query is video-led. Profile the videos, not just the pages.

## Outlier workflow

1. **Flag outliers.** For each channel in the pack, compute its average views from the public channel or video pages, or from a user-provided export. Flag any video with **≥2x** the channel average as an outlier worth studying. Show the arithmetic, for example "48,000 views ÷ 21,000 channel average = 2.3x → outlier". Label it Calculated, with the inputs labeled by source.
2. **Extract packaging patterns.** Read the outlier titles for the format that earned the views. Examples: "X, Clearly Explained", "Stop doing X, do Y instead", and number or year-comparison hooks. These are proven title templates to mirror.
3. **Treat YouTube as a GEO surface.** YouTube videos, their transcripts and their descriptions are AI-citation sources, and a strong video can win the answer even when the page does not. Put video opportunities in the SERP Feature Strategy, not only organic pages.

## Why YouTube matters for GEO

- Google surfaces YouTube in the main SERP, in video mode and in Discover, so a ranking video earns two surfaces at once.
- The OtterlyAI YouTube Citation Study (2026) reports that YouTube and Reddit together account for about 78% of social-media citations in AI Overviews. It also reports that YouTube citations rose about 25% since January 2025, led by instructional and visual-demo content, with long-form videos dominating.
- Treat these as reported figures, not guarantees. AI surfaces shift fast.

## What to recommend for a video opportunity

- **Title**: under about 60 characters, primary keyword in the first few words, and lead with the payoff or a curiosity gap. No keyword stuffing and no ALL CAPS, except one emphasized word.
- **Thumbnail**: high contrast, bold readable type, one clear subject. It should not repeat the title text; add a complementary idea instead.
- **Description**: primary and secondary keyword in the first 2-3 lines, a plain-language summary, and relevant links only.
- **Chapters**: timestamps in the description. The first must be `0:00`, there must be at least three, each at least 10 seconds long, and each title names the sub-topic in searchable words.
- **Transcript/captions**: upload an accurate caption file instead of relying on auto-captions. This gives AI systems quotable passages.
- **Spoken hook**: state the topic and primary keyword out loud in the first 15-30 seconds.
- **Length**: long-form tends to dominate AI citations for instructional topics, so favor depth over padding there. Shorts handle discovery.
- **Tags**: a minor signal. Use a small relevant set.

CTR and average view duration are the signals that compound. Packaging earns the click; the video earns the watch time.
