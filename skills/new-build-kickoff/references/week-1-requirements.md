# Week 1 requirements checklist

The build needs every item below settled by the end of the Kickoff Call. Kickoff assesses each one against the Sales transcript(s), the site and the SOW, and asks the client only about items that are still open.

| ID | Requirement | Usually answered by |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Services offered, and which ones matter most to grow | transcript, site |
| R2 | Customer types: who buys, and any split (residential/commercial, segments, industries) | transcript |
| R3 | Service area: cities or regions served | transcript, site |
| R4 | Main goal of the site and the primary conversion action (call, form, booking) | transcript |
| R5 | Differentiators: why customers choose them, stated specifically | transcript, site |
| R6 | Competitors: 2 or 3 names or URLs | transcript, user |
| R7 | Pages in scope: SOW page count and page list | SOW |
| R8 | Current site: pages or URLs that must be kept or redirected (`n/a` when there is no site) | transcript |
| R9 | Launch timing or hard deadlines | transcript |
| R10 | Proof points beyond testimonials: concrete results, numbers, guarantees | transcript, site |

## Status values

Each row in the `## Checklist Coverage` table of `<slug>-gap-questions.md` gets exactly one status:

- `covered` — the sources answer it specifically. Name the source.
- `asked Qn` — question `n` asks it (an open item, or a confirmation question under rule 3 below).
- `call` — open, and left for the Kickoff Call because 5 questions are already used, or because only a live conversation can settle it.
- `n/a` — does not apply (R8 with no website).

R7 comes from the SOW: `covered` when the SOW states a page count, otherwise `call`, and the context says so.

## Never ask (the intake form collects these)

Contact details; business name, address, phone and hours (NAP); platform logins or access; logo, brand files, colors, fonts; photos or video; testimonials or reviews; team members or bios; certifications, awards, licenses, memberships; legal or compliance language; sites they like or things to avoid.

## Picking the questions

1. Ask about `R` items that no source answers, in table order, up to 5.
2. A vague answer counts as open (for example "we're honest" does not settle R5).
3. If fewer than 3 items are open, add confirmation questions for items backed by only one source, until there are 3.
4. One topic per question. Plain words a business owner would use; no SEO jargon.
5. Do not use the never-ask words in a question even to exclude them ("beyond reviews and testimonials, ..."); the checker rejects them. Ask for results, numbers or guarantees directly.
