## Business and Market Overview

Larkspur Family Dental is a family dental practice founded in 2009 by Dr. Maya Okafor. Three dentists and nine hygienists and assistants work from one office on Larkspur Lane in Boise. The practice offers family and preventive dentistry, emergency visits, dental implants, sedation dentistry, pediatric dentistry, Invisalign and whitening. It serves families in Boise and Meridian, with Boise as the core area and Meridian as the secondary one. The practice has no website today, although the domain larkspurdental.com is registered.

Two groups of patients matter most: families with young kids in Boise and Meridian, and adults who have avoided the dentist for years because of anxiety. Many patients are anxious about dental visits, so a calm, reassuring tone runs through everything we plan. On the kickoff call, the priority order for services was emergency visits first, then implants, then family dentistry. Emergency visits are already the fastest-growing part of the practice.

The practice's main goal for the site is more new-patient appointment requests, especially from emergency and implant patients. The site's main conversions are an online booking request and a phone call. The practice has also shared two goals of its own: to be the top dentist on Google in Boise within a month of launch, and to add twenty more new patients a month by next summer. We treat these as the practice's goals. This plan is built to move toward them, and it does not promise either result or either date.

The practice also asked on the call for a twenty-page site with a page per insurance plan. The agreed scope is a site of up to 12 pages, and the sitemap in this plan follows that scope.

As a dental practice, the site operates under the Idaho State Board of Dentistry and advertising rules, so claims about outcomes are kept factual and reviewed by Dr. Okafor before they go live.

Treasure Valley Smiles leads local search on rankings and links, and Boise Bright Dental leads on AI citations.

**What to learn from them.** Treasure Valley Smiles has a page for every treatment and 64 published reviews on its implant page. Boise Bright Dental answers cost questions directly, which is why AI answers cite it.

**Where the openings are.** No competitor has a page for emergency dental care; all three send people searching for an emergency dentist in Boise to their home page. North End Family Dental has no page for sedation or anxious patients. Larkspur can lead on both topics, which match its strongest services, and can add an implant cost guide to compete for AI citations.

## Strategic Positioning

**Positioning.** The site is built around one positioning statement:

> Positioning: For Boise and Meridian families and anxious adults, Larkspur Family Dental is the calm, same-day family practice that treats emergencies and implants under one roof.

The practice describes its mission this way:

> "Dentistry that people stop dreading."

**What leads the homepage.** The strongest differentiators are:

> Same-day emergency appointments every weekday
> Sedation options for anxious patients
> Implants placed and restored in the same office

Same-day emergency care leads the homepage, because it is the fastest-growing service and the topic no competitor has a page for. Dr. Okafor put the opening in her own words:

> When someone cracks a tooth on a Saturday night, I want us to be the first name they find.

Sedation leads the pages for anxious patients, and implants are backed by the same-office point on the implants page. The proof point available now is the approved claim:

> 4.9 average rating from 212 Google reviews

It belongs on the homepage and the booking page. Gaps in proof remain: no awards or press have been found, and prices have not been supplied.

**Who the site speaks to.** The two personas are the anxious adult, who wants care without fear, and the busy parent, who wants one practice for the whole family. Dr. Okafor described the first group like this:

> Half our new patients tell us they hadn't seen a dentist in five years because they were scared.

and the second like this:

> I'd love for kids to actually look forward to coming in.

**Voice.** The voice is calm, clear and warm. In Dr. Okafor's words:

> We never want to sound like a used-car lot. Calm, clear, no pressure.

In practice that means no pressure words or countdowns, plain language, and reassurance before explanation. On sedation she asked for the same approach:

> If the site can just explain sedation without making it scary, that's a win.

**What not to compete on.** We will not compete on superlatives or guarantees. Claims such as "painless" or "best" are avoided, and the site says what the practice does instead. The head search term "dentist boise" is a longer-term goal, not a launch promise.

## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1: the new site (build)

The new site gives the practice its first search presence. It launches with a dedicated page for each Tier 1 keyword, following the agreed scope of 12 pages and the sitemap above. Emergency dentistry and family dentistry are the first priorities, because both are low-difficulty searches where the research found a real opening. Emergency dentistry is where no competitor has a dedicated page. Sedation and implant pages follow, since sedation serves anxious patients and implants are the highest-value service. Each page carries a clear booking request and phone call-to-action, the practice's two main conversions.

### Phase 2: months 1 to 3

After launch, the focus moves to Tier 2. The head search "dentist boise" is the biggest prize but needs authority the domain does not have yet, so the service pages come first. The three-month content calendar in the keyword research covers the cracked-tooth guide, the implant cost guide and an explainer on whether dental x-rays are safe. The cracked-tooth guide has the lowest difficulty in the set, and the implant cost guide supports the implants page and competes for the AI citations Boise Bright Dental holds today. Blog writing is outside the current scope, so these pages need a separate agreement or an in-house writer.

### Phase 3: months 4 to 6

This phase works on Tier 3 and on the authority gaps the competitor comparison shows. The new domain has far fewer referring domains than every competitor, so earning local links is the main long-term task. The practice's 4.9 average rating from 212 Google reviews is a strength to keep current and visible, while Treasure Valley Smiles shows how reviews on a service page can build trust. Consistent business listings and Google Business Profile upkeep support the same goal.

### Trajectory at 3, 6 and 12 months (directional)

These are directions, not forecasts. We do not promise rankings, positions, traffic or patient numbers.

- **3 months:** the new pages should be indexed and begin to appear for the lower-difficulty local searches, with the emergency, family dentistry and sedation pages first. Booking requests and calls should start to be traceable to the site.
- **6 months:** content from the calendar and the first local links should widen the set of searches the site appears for, and the site should start to be cited in answers to cost questions.
- **12 months:** the site should be a steady source of new-patient enquiries, and the harder searches, including the head term "dentist boise", become realistic to pursue as authority grows.

The goal of being the top dentist on Google in Boise within a month of launch is not something the sources support. A new domain has to earn its position, and the figures above show the gap to close.
