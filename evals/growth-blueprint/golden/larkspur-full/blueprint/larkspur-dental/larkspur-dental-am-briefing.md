## Executive Summary

- Emergency care is the opening: no competitor has a dedicated page, and "emergency dentist boise" has 1,300 monthly searches.
- The practice starts from zero against Treasure Valley Smiles, which holds 54.0% of estimated traffic.
- The sitemap uses 11 counted pages against the 12-page scope.

- The client owes team photos (November), prices and approvals.

## Key Strategic Bets

1. Lead with emergency and sedation pages. Reason: competitors have no page for either. Wrong if: the pages do not rank or draw calls within the first months.
2. Build service pages first and chase "dentist boise" later. Reason: the domain has a Domain Rating of 9 and needs authority. Wrong if: service pages stall and the head term stays out of reach.
3. Close the link gap with local links and reviews. Reason: 14 referring domains against 312 for the leader. Wrong if: links do not come in by months 4 to 6.

## Anticipated Client Questions

1. Q: Why only 11 counted pages when we asked for a twenty-page site with a page per insurance plan?
   A: The agreed scope is up to 12 pages, and the sitemap follows it. Extra pages are outside the scope.
2. Q: Can we be the top dentist on Google in Boise within a month of launch?
   A: We cannot promise that. A new domain with a Domain Rating of 9 has to earn its position.
3. Q: Why is "dentist boise" not in month one?
   A: It has 6,600 monthly searches and difficulty 61, so we build service pages first.
4. Q: How will we reach twenty more new patients a month?
   A: That is the practice's goal, not a commitment. We track booking requests and calls to measure progress.
5. Q: What do you need from us?
   A: Team photos in November, prices and sign-off on clinical claims and the disclaimer.

## Calibration Framing

Trajectory is directional only: no promised rankings, traffic or patient numbers. Blog writing is excluded from the scope, so the content calendar needs a separate agreement. The twenty-page request and the one-month ranking goal are not covered; redirect both politely. Also flag internally: the sitemap excludes the home and contact pages from its count of 11, while the scope describes the 12 pages as including them. Confirm with SEO before the call.
