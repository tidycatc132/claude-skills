## Evidence pack (verified by Dana, use these and only these numbers)
- U.S. Department of Energy, "Tankless or Demand-Type Water Heaters" (energy.gov/energysaver/tankless-or-demand-type-water-heaters): most tankless water heaters have a life expectancy of more than 20 years; storage water heaters last 10 to 15 years.
- U.S. Department of Energy, same page: for homes that use 41 gallons or less of hot water daily, demand water heaters can be 24% to 34% more energy efficient than conventional storage tank water heaters.
- Portland Water Bureau, "Water Quality" page (portland.gov/water/water-quality): Bull Run water is very soft, typically around 3 to 8 parts per million (ppm) of hardness.
- Rinnai residential tankless warranty (rinnai.us/warranty): 12-year heat exchanger warranty on most residential condensing models when installed and maintained per the manual.
- Harbor & Pine job data, 2025 (first-hand, cite as Harbor & Pine service records): 412 tank water heaters replaced; average age at replacement 11.6 years. 96 tankless units descaled; units on well water in Clackamas County averaged 3.1 times more scale by weight than units on Portland city water.
- Harbor & Pine pricing (approved for publication): tankless water heater replacement in Portland starts at $3,850 installed for a like-for-like swap; switching from a tank water heater to tankless starts at $5,400 because of gas line and venting changes.

## Expert quote (approved)
"On Portland city water I tell customers to descale every two years. On a Clackamas well, it's every year, no exceptions, or the heat exchanger pays for it." (Marcus Delgado, master plumber, Harbor & Pine Plumbing, interview with Dana Whitfield, 2026-09-30)
