# How Much Does Water Heater Repair Cost in Snoqualmie?

By [Marcus Hale](/about/), licensed plumber · Updated 2026-10-15

Water heater repair from Bramblewood Plumbing costs between $180 and $650 for most tank repairs, according to Bramblewood Plumbing's 2025 job records. A written price is given before work starts.

## Key Takeaways

- Bramblewood Plumbing repairs gas and electric tank water heaters.
- Bramblewood Plumbing gives a written price before any repair starts.
- A water heater older than 12 years is usually replaced rather than repaired.

## What water heater problems does Bramblewood Plumbing repair?

Bramblewood Plumbing repairs pilot and igniter faults, failed heating elements, leaking valves and thermostats. Each repair starts with a test of the unit.

## When is a water heater worth repairing instead of replacing?

A water heater is worth repairing when it is under 10 years old and the tank itself does not leak, according to the U.S. Department of Energy.
