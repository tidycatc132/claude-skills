# What Does Drain Cleaning Cost in North Bend?

By [Marcus Hale](/about/), licensed plumber · Updated 2026-10-15

Drain cleaning from Bramblewood Plumbing costs $145 for a single sink, tub or shower line and $295 for a main sewer line, according to Bramblewood Plumbing's 2026 price list. Camera inspection is quoted separately.

## Key Takeaways

- Bramblewood Plumbing clears sink, tub, shower and main sewer lines.
- Bramblewood Plumbing charges $145 for a single fixture line.
- Bramblewood Plumbing offers camera inspection for repeat clogs.

## Which drains does Bramblewood Plumbing clear?

Bramblewood Plumbing clears kitchen and bathroom sink lines, tub and shower drains, floor drains and main sewer lines. Main lines are cleared with a cable machine.

## What causes repeat clogs in North Bend homes?

Repeat clogs in North Bend homes usually come from grease in kitchen lines and roots in older clay sewer pipes, according to Bramblewood Plumbing's 2025 job records.

## When does a drain need a camera inspection?

A drain needs a camera inspection when it clogs again within 3 months of cleaning. Bramblewood Plumbing records the inspection and gives the customer the video.

## Can I clear a drain myself?

A homeowner can clear a slow sink with a plunger or a hand auger. Chemical drain cleaners can damage older pipes, according to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency.
