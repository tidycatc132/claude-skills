# How Does Ant Control Work in Maple Falls?

By [Dana Okafor](/about/), licensed pest technician · Updated 2026-10-24

Ant control from Hollis Creek Pest Control starts with an inspection that finds the nest, then a bait treatment placed along the trails. A standard ant treatment costs $165 for a home under 2,500 square feet, according to Hollis Creek Pest Control's 2026 price list.

## Which ants does Hollis Creek Pest Control treat?

Hollis Creek Pest Control treats carpenter ants, pavement ants and odorous house ants. Carpenter ants get a separate inspection of wet wood because they nest inside damaged framing.

## How long does an ant treatment take to work?

Most ant trails stop within 10 days of a bait treatment, according to Hollis Creek Pest Control's 2025 service records. Carpenter ant colonies can take up to 3 weeks.

## Is ant bait safe around pets?

Hollis Creek Pest Control places bait in closed stations out of reach of pets. The technician lists every product used on the service ticket.
