# What Does Rodent Removal Include?

By [Dana Okafor](/about/), licensed pest technician · Updated 2026-10-24

Rodent removal from Hollis Creek Pest Control includes an inspection, trapping over two visits and sealing the gaps mice and rats use to get in. The full service costs $340 for most homes, according to Hollis Creek Pest Control's 2026 price list.

## Key Takeaways

- Rodent removal covers inspection, two trapping visits and exclusion work.
- The full service costs $340 for most homes.
- Hollis Creek Pest Control seals gaps with steel mesh and sealant.

## How does Hollis Creek Pest Control find where rodents get in?

The technician checks the foundation, vents, garage door seals and roof line for gaps wider than a pencil. Each gap is photographed and marked on the service ticket.

## How many visits does rodent removal take?

Rodent removal takes two trapping visits a week apart, then a final check. Most homes are clear after the second visit, according to Hollis Creek Pest Control's 2025 service records.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Hollis Creek Pest Control use poison for rodents?

Hollis Creek Pest Control uses traps inside the home and does not place poison indoors.
