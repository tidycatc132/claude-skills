# How Often Should Dogs Get Their Teeth Cleaned?

By [Dr Hana Ellery](/about-us/), BVSc MRCVS · Updated 2026-10-15

Most adult dogs need a professional teeth cleaning every 12 months, according to the British Veterinary Dental Association. Small breeds often need one sooner.

## Key Takeaways

- Most adult dogs need a professional cleaning every 12 months.
- Small breeds often need cleanings more often.
- Daily brushing lengthens the time between cleanings.

## Why do small dogs need more frequent cleanings?

Small dogs need more frequent cleanings because their teeth sit closer together and trap more plaque, according to the British Veterinary Dental Association.

## Does brushing replace professional cleaning?

Brushing does not replace professional cleaning, because brushing cannot reach below the gum line. Daily brushing slows plaque build-up.

## What are the signs a dog's teeth need attention?

The signs a dog's teeth need attention are bad breath, red or bleeding gums, loose teeth and dropping food.

## What does a dental chew do?

A dental chew scrapes some plaque from the tooth surface. [NEEDS SOURCE: study on dental chews and plaque reduction]

## Frequently Asked Questions

### At what age should a dog have its first cleaning?

Most dogs have their first professional cleaning between ages 2 and 3, according to the British Veterinary Dental Association.

### Is anaesthetic safe for older dogs?

Anaesthetic is used for older dogs after a pre-anaesthetic blood test, according to Oakmere Veterinary Clinic's clinical policy.
