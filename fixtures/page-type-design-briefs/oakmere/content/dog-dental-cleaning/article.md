# How Much Does Dog Dental Cleaning Cost at Oakmere Veterinary Clinic?

By [Dr Hana Ellery](/about-us/), BVSc MRCVS · Updated 2026-10-15

Dog dental cleaning at Oakmere Veterinary Clinic costs from £260 under general anaesthetic, according to Oakmere Veterinary Clinic's 2026 price list. Extractions are priced after the X-rays.

## Key Takeaways

- Oakmere Veterinary Clinic cleans dogs' teeth under general anaesthetic.
- Oakmere Veterinary Clinic prices a cleaning from £260.

## What happens during a dog dental cleaning?

A dog dental cleaning at Oakmere Veterinary Clinic is a scale and polish under general anaesthetic, with dental X-rays. The dog goes home the same day.

## How do I know my dog needs a dental cleaning?

A dog needs a dental check when it has bad breath, red gums or drops food, according to the British Veterinary Dental Association.
