### Repeating Blocks

- Every service page repeats the Opening answer, Key Takeaways, FAQ and Booking with the same job.
- Inside one page, each question section repeats: a question heading and its answer.
- Each FAQ item repeats: a short question and a two-sentence answer.

### Content Length

Words: 360 to 407 (2 pages)
H2 sections: 6 to 8

- The number of question sections varies from 3 to 5; the template holds both ends without empty space.
- FAQ items stay at two per page.

### CTAs

- The phone number sits with the Opening answer, so a visitor can call before reading on [article].
- The Booking section closes every page with the phone number and the contact form link [article].

- Navy `#1B2A41` for headings, the header and the footer [kit: brand.md]

- Slate `#5B6770` for body text [kit: brand.md]
- Headings in Barlow, body text in Inter [kit: brand.md]

### Responsive Priorities

- On mobile the phone number and the Opening answer come first [article].
