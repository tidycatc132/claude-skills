# Panel Upgrades in Larchmont: When You Need One and What It Costs

Most Larchmont homes built before 1970 still run on a 100-amp panel. A panel upgrade replaces it with a 200-amp panel so a heat pump, an induction range or an EV charger can run at the same time without tripping breakers. Larchmont Electric quotes the job after a free visit, pulls the permit and books the utility disconnect.

## Key Takeaways

- A 100-amp panel is the usual limit in older Larchmont homes.
- Most upgrades take one day on site, plus the utility appointment.
- The Westchester permit and inspection are part of the quote.

## When does a home need a panel upgrade?

A home needs a panel upgrade when the loads it runs at once add up to more than the panel can carry. The common signs are breakers that trip when two large appliances run together, a panel with no free slots, a fuse box, and plans for a heat pump or EV charger. A load calculation during the free visit shows whether the panel can carry the new load or not.

## How long does a panel upgrade take?

The work on site takes one day. Power is off for about six hours while the crew swaps the panel and reconnects each circuit. The utility has to disconnect and reconnect the service, so the date depends on their schedule, usually one to two weeks after the permit is approved.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to be home?

Someone needs to let the crew in and be reachable. The power is off for most of the day.

### Will my insurance company want to know?

Many insurers ask about fuse boxes and older panels. The inspection certificate is the document they ask for.
