# EV Charger Installation in Larchmont and New Rochelle

A Level 2 charger in the garage or driveway adds about 25 miles of range per hour of charging. Larchmont Electric installs hardwired and plug-in chargers, checks whether the panel can carry the load first, and handles the permit.

## Key Takeaways

- A load calculation comes before any charger is installed.
- Most installs are done in half a day.
- Hardwired chargers need a permit in Westchester.

## Which EV charger fits my home?

The charger that fits is the one the panel and the car can both use. Most cars accept 32 to 48 amps on a Level 2 charger. A 40-amp circuit is the common choice because it suits most cars and leaves room on a 200-amp panel.

## Do I need a permit for an EV charger?

A hardwired charger needs an electrical permit and an inspection in Westchester. A plug-in charger on a new 240-volt outlet needs one too, because the outlet is a new circuit.

## Can my panel handle an EV charger?

Many 100-amp panels cannot carry a 40-amp charger alongside the rest of the house. The load calculation answers this before the install is booked. If the panel cannot carry it, the options are a smaller circuit, a load-management device, or a [panel upgrade](/services/panel-upgrades/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you install a charger I already bought?

Yes, if it is listed for home use. Bring the model number to the visit.

### Does the charger work in winter?

Yes. Chargers are rated for outdoor use; charging is slower in deep cold.
