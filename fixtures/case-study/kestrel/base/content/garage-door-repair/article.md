# Garage Door Repair in Boise, ID

A garage door that sticks, shakes or comes off its track is more than a nuisance. Most repairs we do in Boise take under two hours, and we quote the price on the phone first.

## Common repairs

Frayed cables, worn rollers, bent tracks and dented panels. If the door will not open at all, it is often a [broken spring](/services/spring-replacement/).

## When to replace instead

If more than two panels are damaged or the door is older than twenty years, a new door can cost less over time. We will tell you honestly.
