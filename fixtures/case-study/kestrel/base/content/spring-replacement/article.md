# Garage Door Spring Replacement in Boise

A loud bang from the garage and a door that will not lift usually means a spring has snapped. Springs hold the weight of the door, so do not try to lift it by hand or replace the spring yourself.

## Same-day spring replacement

Call before noon and we can usually replace the spring the same day in Boise, Meridian or Nampa. We replace springs in pairs so the door stays balanced.

## What it costs

We quote the full price on the phone, including parts and labour, before we send a technician.
