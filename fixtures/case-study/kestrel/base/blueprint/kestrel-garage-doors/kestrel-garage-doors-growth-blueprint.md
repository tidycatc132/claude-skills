# Kestrel Garage Doors: Growth Blueprint

Client: Kestrel Garage Doors
Date: 2026-10-05
Prepared by: WISE Digital Partners

## Executive Summary

- **Repair calls are the way in.** Spring and repair searches carry most of the local demand, and "garage door repair boise" has 1,300 monthly searches.
- **Two towns with no strong local page.** Nobody has a page that answers Meridian or Nampa searches well, so the build adds a page for each.
- **A site sized to the agreed scope.** The plan uses 9 counted pages against the 10-page scope.

## Business and Market Overview

Kestrel Garage Doors is a family-run garage door company founded in 2012 by Gus Halvorsen, with a crew of seven technicians in Boise. Most new customers find Kestrel when a spring breaks and the car is stuck inside, and the owners want those repair calls to grow in Meridian and Nampa.

The current site has 6 pages, no page per service and no page for either town. The phone number sits only in the footer and the site is hard to use on a phone, which matters because most repair calls are made from a phone in the driveway.

## Competitive Analysis

Treasure Valley Overhead Door and Gem State Door Co. both rank for Boise repair terms with one general services page each. Neither has a page for Meridian or Nampa.

Same-day spring replacement and the upfront phone price lead every page.

## Keyword Opportunity

Tier 1 keywords are the main keywords of the build pages: garage door repair boise, garage door spring replacement boise, garage door installation boise, garage door opener installation boise, garage door repair meridian and garage door repair nampa.

## Site Architecture and Content Plan

One page per service, one page each for Meridian and Nampa, and the phone number in the header of every page.

Source: seo-reports/site-structure-optimizer/2026-10-02-kestrel-garage-doors.md

## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1

Build the 9 pages, with repair and spring pages first.

### Phase 2

Months 1 to 3 after launch: reviews, Google Business Profile posts and the first guide on garage door noise.

### Phase 3

Months 4 to 6: more location pages if Meridian and Nampa pages earn calls.

### Trajectory

Repair and spring calls from Meridian and Nampa should rise first, then installation enquiries.
