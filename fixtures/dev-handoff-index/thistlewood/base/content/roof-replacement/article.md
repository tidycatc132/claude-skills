# Roof Replacement in Redmond, OR

A full roof replacement in Redmond usually takes two to three days for a typical home. Not every worn roof needs one: a [roof repair](/services/roof-repair/) is often enough when the decking is sound.

## How much does a roof replacement cost in Redmond?

Most asphalt replacements we quote fall between $11,000 and $19,000 ([NRCA cost guide](https://www.nrca.net/)).

## When is a repair enough?

If the leak is local and the shingles are under 15 years old, a repair usually is. See our [past projects](/gallery/) for examples.
