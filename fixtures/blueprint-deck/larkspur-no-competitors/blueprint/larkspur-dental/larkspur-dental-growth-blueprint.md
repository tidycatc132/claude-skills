## Business and Market Overview

Larkspur Family Dental is a family practice founded in 2009 by Dr. Maya Okafor. Three dentists and nine hygienists and assistants work from one office on Larkspur Lane in Boise. The practice serves families in Boise and Meridian, Idaho, with Boise as the core area and Meridian as the secondary area. There is no website today; the domain larkspurdental.com is registered.

**Services.** Family and preventive dentistry, emergency visits, dental implants, sedation dentistry, pediatric dentistry, Invisalign and whitening. You told us the order that matters most: emergency visits first, then implants, then family dentistry.

**Who we are writing for.** Two audiences came out of the kickoff call: families with young kids in Boise and Meridian, and adults who have avoided the dentist for years because of anxiety.

**What you want from the site.** More new-patient appointment requests, especially emergency and implant patients. The main actions we will build around are an online booking request and a phone call. On the call you shared two goals of your own: to be the top dentist on Google in Boise within a month of launch, and twenty more new patients a month by next summer. We treat these as your goals. Search results depend on factors outside anyone's control, so we do not promise a ranking or a patient number, and the plan below describes direction rather than guarantees.

**A regulated field.** Dental advertising is held to honest-claims rules, so the site avoids words like "painless" and "guaranteed" and uses only ratings and proof points that are real and current.

## Strategic Positioning

**Our positioning.**

> For Boise and Meridian families and anxious adults, Larkspur Family Dental is the calm, same-day family practice that treats emergencies and implants under one roof.

The site is built to say this in the first screen of the homepage and to keep saying it on every page.

**What leads.** Your differentiators, in the order they will lead the site:

- Same-day emergency appointments every weekday. This leads the homepage and owns the emergency page. In your words:

> We see emergencies the same day, every weekday, no exceptions.

- Sedation options for anxious patients. This leads the sedation page and the New Patients page. You asked for the site to explain sedation without making it scary:

> If the site can just explain sedation without making it scary, that's a win.

- Implants placed and restored in the same office. This leads the implants page, which answers what you said most people do not know:

> Implants change people's lives, and most people don't know we do them.

**Proof.** The approved proof points are "4.9 average rating from 212 Google reviews" and "Same-day emergency appointments on weekdays". They back the homepage, the emergency page and the New Patients page. No awards or press have been found, so we do not lean on them.

**Who it speaks to.** Dana, the anxious adult, searches on a phone, often after hours, and wants care without fear. Chris, the busy parent, wants one practice for the whole family. Your own words for the first group:

> Half our new patients tell us they hadn't seen a dentist in five years because they were scared.

**Voice.** You set the standard yourself:

> We never want to sound like a used-car lot. Calm, clear, no pressure.

In practice that means reassuring before explaining, plain words, and warmth without baby talk.

**What not to compete on.** We do not claim to be the "best dentist in Boise" and we do not promise painless results. We say what the practice does instead.

## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1: the new site (build)

The new site gives the practice its first online home. It launches with a page for each service you want to be found for, matched to the Tier 1 keywords above, plus a Meridian page for your second service area, About and Our Team pages, a New Patients page and a Contact page. Emergency care leads, then implants and family dentistry, in the order you gave us. Every page carries a clear way to book or call.

The sitemap follows the agreed scope of 12 pages. You mentioned on the call a twenty-page site with a page per insurance plan. That is outside the current scope, so it is not in this plan.

### Phase 2: months 1 to 3

With the core pages live, the focus moves to the Tier 2 keywords: "what to do for a cracked tooth" (2,900 searches a month, difficulty 17), "how much do dental implants cost" (9,900 searches, difficulty 48) and, as the long game, "dentist boise". The keyword research sets out a three-month content calendar: a cracked-tooth explainer, an implant cost guide that supports the implants page, and an explainer on whether dental x-rays are safe. Blog writing is outside the current agreement, so the calendar is a plan to agree on rather than work already included.

### Phase 3: months 4 to 6

This phase builds the long-term authority that the biggest search terms need. The Tier 3 keyword is "are dental x rays safe", a common new-patient question. The competitor review, once it is saved, will show where links, reviews and local listings can close gaps; until then we do not name specific authority targets.

### Trajectory at 3, 6 and 12 months (directional)

These are directional. They describe what should start to move, not a promise of rankings, traffic or patient numbers.

- **3 months:** the new pages are found and indexed, and the emergency, family dentistry and sedation pages, which target the lowest-difficulty keywords, should be the first to show movement. Booking requests and calls begin to come from search.
- **6 months:** the implants, pediatric and Meridian pages should gain visibility as the Tier 2 content supports them, and new reviews keep adding to your rating.
- **12 months:** the site should be competing for the broader terms such as "dentist boise", which need the authority built in the earlier phases, and search should be a steady source of new-patient requests, especially emergency and implant patients.
