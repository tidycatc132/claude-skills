# A calm new website for a Boise family dental practice, with a page for every service

## Client Snapshot

- Client: Larkspur Family Dental
- Website: https://larkspurdental.com
- Service area: Boise and Meridian, Idaho
- Launched: 2026-11-20
- Old site: none
- Build: 13 pages

Larkspur Family Dental is a family practice on Larkspur Lane in Boise, founded by Dr. Maya Okafor. Its team serves families with young kids in Boise and Meridian and adults who have put off the dentist because of anxiety.

## The Challenge

The old site had a few pages the practice had stopped updating, with no page per service and no page for Meridian. The practice wanted more new-patient appointment requests, above all from emergency and implant patients, but people searching for an emergency dentist in Boise had no Larkspur page to find.

## The Approach

We positioned Larkspur as the calm, same-day family practice that treats emergencies and implants under one roof. The build put emergency and family dentistry first, because both are searches where the research found a real opening, then sedation for anxious patients and implants as the highest-value service. Every page carries a booking request and a phone call to action.

## What We Built

- 13 pages: 1 homepage, 7 service pages, 1 location page, 3 About pages and 1 Contact page
- 1,024 words of new page copy across those pages
- 2 target keywords mapped to the pages
- 3 old URLs redirected to their new pages

The new site has its own Emergency Dentist page, which explains what counts as an emergency and what happens at the visit, plus pages for Dental Implants, Sedation Dentistry, Pediatric Dentistry, Invisalign and Teeth Whitening, a Meridian page for nearby families and a New Patients page that sets out the first visit.

## Results

It is too early to report results: the site launched on 2026-11-20. At 90 days we will measure online booking requests and phone calls, rankings for the keywords each page targets, and enquiries from emergency and implant patients.
