## Strengths to Learn From

- Treasure Valley Smiles has a service page for every treatment and 64 published reviews on its implant page.
- Boise Bright Dental answers cost questions directly, which is why AI answers cite it.

## Weaknesses to Exploit

- No competitor has a page for emergency dental care; all three send "emergency dentist boise" searchers to the home page.
- North End Family Dental has no page for sedation or anxious patients.

## Priority Opportunities

1. Emergency dental care page (no competitor page in the top 5).
2. Sedation dentistry page for anxious patients.
3. Implant cost guide to win AI citations from Boise Bright Dental.

## Action Plan

- Immediate: emergency and sedation pages in the build.
- Short-term: implant cost guide.
- Long-term: earn local links to close the referring-domain gap.
