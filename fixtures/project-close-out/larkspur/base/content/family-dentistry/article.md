# Family Dentistry in Boise

One office for the whole family: checkups, cleanings and fillings for children, teens, adults and grandparents.

## Checkups and cleanings

Twice-yearly visits catch small problems before they become painful ones. Our hygienists clean, polish and check for gum disease at every visit.

## Fillings and crowns

Tooth-coloured fillings and same-office crowns, explained before we begin.

## Booking the family together

Ask the front desk for back-to-back appointments so the whole family is seen in one trip.
