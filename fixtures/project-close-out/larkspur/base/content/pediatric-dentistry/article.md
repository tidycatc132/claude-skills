# Pediatric Dentistry in Boise

A child's first visits shape how they feel about the dentist for life. We keep them short, gentle and fun.

## The first visit

We recommend a first visit by your child's first birthday. We count teeth, check the gums and show you how to brush.

## Sealants and fluoride

Sealants protect the chewing surfaces of back teeth, and fluoride varnish strengthens enamel. Both take minutes.
