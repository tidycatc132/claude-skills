# Sedation Dentistry in Boise

If dental visits make you anxious, sedation can help you relax through cleanings and treatment.

## Options we offer

Nitrous oxide, known as laughing gas, wears off in minutes. Oral sedation is a pill taken before your visit; you will need someone to drive you home.

## Is sedation right for me

We talk through your health history and what worries you most, then suggest the lightest option that works.
