# Emergency Dentist in Boise

A cracked tooth, a knocked-out tooth or a sudden toothache can't wait. Larkspur Family Dental keeps time open every weekday for emergency visits.

## What counts as a dental emergency

Severe pain, swelling in the face or jaw, a tooth knocked out or loose, a broken crown or filling, or bleeding that won't stop. If you have trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the emergency room first.

## What happens at your visit

We take an x-ray, find the cause of the pain and tell you the options and the price before treatment. Most visits end with you out of pain the same day.

## What to do before you arrive

Rinse with warm water, keep a knocked-out tooth in milk and bring it with you, and take an over-the-counter pain reliever if you can.
