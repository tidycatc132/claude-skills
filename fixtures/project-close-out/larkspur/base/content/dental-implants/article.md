# Dental Implants in Boise

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth with a post and crown that look and work like your own tooth.

## Who implants are for

Adults missing one or more teeth with healthy gums and enough jawbone. We check both at a consultation.

## How the process works

We place the post, give it time to heal, then fit the crown. Most patients need three or four visits over a few months.

## What implants cost

The price depends on how many teeth, whether bone grafting is needed and the crown. We give you a written estimate at the consultation.
