# Dentist for Meridian Families

Larkspur Family Dental is a short drive from Meridian along the Interstate, and many of our families come from Meridian neighbourhoods.

## Services for Meridian patients

Family checkups, emergency visits, implants, sedation and children's dentistry, all in one office.

## Getting here

Take the Interstate east to the Cole Road exit; we are two minutes from the exit with free parking out front.
