# Calm Family Dentistry in Boise and Meridian

Larkspur Family Dental has cared for Boise families since 2009. Dr. Maya Okafor and her team see kids, parents and grandparents in one calm office on Larkspur Lane, and we keep same-day time open for dental emergencies.

## Same-day emergency visits

Call before 11 a.m. with a broken tooth, a lost filling or sudden pain and we will do our best to see you that day. We explain what we find and what it costs before we start.

## Care for every age

Cleanings, fillings and checkups for the whole family, a gentle first visit for children, and implants, Invisalign and whitening for adults who want more.

## For anxious patients

Many of our patients had not seen a dentist in years. We go at your pace, explain each step and offer sedation when it helps.

## Book a visit

Request an appointment online or call the front desk. New patients are welcome, and we will tell you what to bring.
