# Larkspur Family Dental: voice

## Voice Dimensions

### Calm, not clinical
Explain what happens next in plain words. Passes: "Here's what the first visit looks like."

### Warm, not gushing
Thank people simply and mean it. Passes: "Thanks, Dana. This was a team effort."

### Clear, not salesy
Say what was done and what comes next, no hype. Passes: "Your new emergency page is live."

## Tone Matrix

| Context | Tone shift | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | Reassuring and plain | "Same-day emergency visits in Boise." |
| Email | Friendly, first names, short paragraphs, no exclamation marks | "Good news, Dana. The new site is live." |
| Support reply | Calm, one next step | "We'll call you back this afternoon." |

## Do / Don't Examples

| Do | Don't | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Same-day emergency visits" | "Instant pain relief" | Only real promises |
| "Boise and Meridian" | "All of Idaho" | Be specific |
| "Sedation for anxious patients" | "Painless dentistry guaranteed" | Board advertising rules |
| "212 Google reviews" | "Everyone loves us" | Use the real proof |
| "Book a visit" | "Act now!" | No pressure |

## Reading Level & Cadence

- Grade 7, average sentence 13 words, max 24, active voice, paragraphs of 1 to 3 sentences.
