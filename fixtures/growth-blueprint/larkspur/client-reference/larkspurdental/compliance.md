## Regulatory Framework
| Body or law | Why it applies to this client | What it means for content |
|---|---|---|
| Idaho State Board of Dentistry | licensed dental practice | no misleading claims about outcomes |
| FTC Act | advertising | reviews and ratings must be real and current |
| HIPAA | patient data | no patient stories without written consent |

## Mandatory Disclaimers
> Results vary from patient to patient. Proposed, needs legal approval. Appears on implant and Invisalign pages.

## Prohibited Claims
- "Painless" or "guaranteed" results; say "comfortable" and "most patients".
- "Best dentist in Boise"; say what the practice does instead.
