Tankless Water Heater Maintenance Tips

Posted March 4, 2019 by Admin

Tankless water heaters are great, but they need some love. Here are our top tips.

Flush it

You should flush your unit regularly. Most people should do it once a year.

Check the filter

There is a small inlet water filter. Clean it.
