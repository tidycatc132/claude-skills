## Verified facts we can publish
- Harbor & Pine service records, 2025: 412 tank water heaters replaced, average age at replacement 11.6 years. 96 tankless units descaled; units on Clackamas County well water averaged 3.1 times more scale by weight than units on Portland city water.
- Approved pricing: tankless water heater replacement in Portland starts at $3,850 installed (like-for-like); tank-to-tankless conversion starts at $5,400 (gas line and venting). Descaling visit: $189 flat.
- Field case, 2025, Oregon City: Navien tankless unit on well water, 6 years old, never descaled, heat exchanger failed.

## Expert quote (approved, interview 2026-09-30)
"On Portland city water I tell customers to descale every two years. On a Clackamas well, it's every year, no exceptions, or the heat exchanger pays for it." (Marcus Delgado)

## External sources Dana checked
- U.S. Department of Energy, "Tankless or Demand-Type Water Heaters" (energy.gov/energysaver/tankless-or-demand-type-water-heaters): most tankless water heaters last more than 20 years; storage water heaters last 10 to 15 years.
- Portland Water Bureau, "Water Quality" (portland.gov/water/water-quality): Bull Run water is very soft, typically around 3 to 8 ppm hardness.
- Rinnai residential warranty (rinnai.us/warranty): 12-year heat exchanger warranty on most residential condensing models when installed and maintained per the manual.
