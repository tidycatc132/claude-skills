# Saltmarsh Window Co.: brand

Industry: residential window and gutter cleaning (home services). Owner: Dana Whitlock, who bought the business in 2018. The business was founded in 2011 by Gil Marsh, who retired in 2018.

## Story
Gil Marsh started Saltmarsh Window Co. in 2011 with one ladder and a pickup truck, cleaning windows on Johns Island. Dana Whitlock worked for Gil for four years and bought the business when he retired in 2018. Today Saltmarsh cleans windows, gutters and screens for homes across the Charleston area.
