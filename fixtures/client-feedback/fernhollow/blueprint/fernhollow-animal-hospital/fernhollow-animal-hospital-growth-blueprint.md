# Fernhollow Animal Hospital: Growth Blueprint

## Executive Summary

Pet owners in Bend searching for urgent care find Pine Butte Veterinary first. The new site leads with emergency and urgent care, where search demand is highest and no local clinic owns the results yet, then builds dental and wellness pages that turn first visits into long-term patients.

Source: kickoff/fernhollow-animal-hospital/fernhollow-animal-hospital-context.md; research/keyword-research/2026-11-20-fernhollow.md

## Business and Market Overview

Fernhollow is a four-veterinarian small-animal hospital in Bend founded in 2014, with about 340 Google reviews at a 4.8 rating. The goal is more new wellness and dental patients.

## Growth Strategy

### Phase 1

Build the eight pages, emergency and urgent care first.

### Phase 2

Months 1 to 3: earn reviews that mention urgent visits and measure calls from the emergency page.

### Phase 3

Months 4 to 6: widen dental and wellness pages once the emergency page ranks.

### Trajectory

Emergency searches should move first, then dental and wellness.
