# Client brief: Ferngully Coffee Roasters

Prepared by account lead, 2026-10-10. This is everything we have so far; the client has not sent a brand book.

- Small-batch specialty coffee roaster in Asheville, North Carolina. Founded 2019 by siblings Rosa and Teo Alvarez.
- Sells online only (Shopify store at ferngullycoffee.example) and to about 40 cafes wholesale. Ships to the United States and Canada.
- Products: single-origin whole-bean coffee in 12 oz bags ($19 to $24), the "Canopy" house espresso blend, and the Ferngully Subscription (a bag every 2 or 4 weeks, 10% off, skip or cancel any time).
- Buys green coffee from three named farms: Finca El Roble (Huila, Colombia), Kiamabara Factory (Nyeri, Kenya) and two smallholder cooperatives in Minas Gerais, Brazil. Roasts on Mondays and Thursdays, ships within 48 hours of roasting.
- Want to be known for: transparency about where the coffee comes from and what the farm was paid.
- Audience they describe: home espresso hobbyists and pour-over people, mostly 25 to 45. Also cafe owners buying wholesale.
- Tone notes from Rosa: "nerdy but never snobby". They hate "artisanal" and "the perfect cup".
- They run an email newsletter and want to grow the subscription with promo codes and a refer-a-friend offer.
- No certifications yet (not organic, not Fair Trade certified). They do publish the price paid per pound on some product pages.
