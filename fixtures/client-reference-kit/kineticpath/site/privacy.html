Website Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 3, 2026

This policy covers information collected through kineticpathpt.com. Protected health information you share with us as a patient is governed by our separate Notice of Privacy Practices under HIPAA.

We use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel on public pages to understand site traffic. We do not place these tools on our booking or patient intake pages, and we do not share protected health information with advertising platforms.

If you submit the contact form, do not include medical details. Use the patient portal for anything about your care.

Ohio residents may contact privacy@kineticpathpt.com with questions.
