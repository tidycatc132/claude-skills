About Kinetic Path

Kinetic Path was founded in 2017 by Dr. Maya Ortiz, DPT, OCS, after a decade working in high-volume clinics where patients saw a therapist for ten minutes and an aide for the rest.

Our mission: give every patient a full hour of expert care and a plan they understand.

Today our team of 14 Doctors of Physical Therapy treats patients at three Columbus clinics.
