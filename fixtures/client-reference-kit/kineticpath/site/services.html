Services

Orthopedic physical therapy

Back, neck, hip, knee, shoulder and ankle pain. We start with a full evaluation and build a plan around your goals.

Post-surgical rehab

ACL reconstruction, rotator cuff repair, total knee and hip replacement. We work with your surgeon's protocol.

Run Lab

Video gait analysis for runners of every level. We film you on the treadmill, break down your stride and give you a written plan with drills and shoe guidance.

PathPlan

An 8-visit program to get you back to the activity you love, with a home program you keep after discharge.

Dry needling

Offered as part of a plan of care when your PT thinks it will help.
