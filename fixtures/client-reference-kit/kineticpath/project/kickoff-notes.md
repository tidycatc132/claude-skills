# Kinetic Path Physical Therapy: kickoff call notes

Date of call: 2026-10-06
Attendees: Dr. Maya Ortiz, DPT (clinical director and owner), Jordan Lee (practice manager), our account team.

## The business
- Three outpatient clinics in Columbus, Ohio: Clintonville, Grandview Heights and Westerville. Opened 2017.
- 14 licensed physical therapists, all DPTs. Two are board-certified orthopaedic clinical specialists (OCS).
- Every visit is one-on-one with a licensed PT for the full 55 minutes. No aides or techs run treatment. This is the main thing Maya wants us to push; most local competitors (big chains) double-book and hand patients off to aides.
- In-network with Aetna, Anthem, Medical Mutual, UnitedHealthcare and Medicare. Out of network for everyone else; they file the claim for the patient.
- Ohio has Direct Access: patients can book without a physician referral. Many people do not know this. Maya wants it in most pages.

## Who they want
- Adults 30 to 65, active or wanting to get back to being active: runners, weekend athletes, people with desk-job back and neck pain, post-surgical knee and shoulder rehab.
- Referral sources they care about: orthopaedic surgeons at OhioHealth and Mount Carmel, and local running clubs (they sponsor the Clintonville Running Club).
- Do not target workers' comp or pediatric; they do not offer either.

## How Maya wants to sound
- "Like a smart friend who happens to be a PT." Warm, direct, plain English.
- No medical jargon without explaining it. Say "your kneecap", not "patellofemoral joint", unless you explain it.
- Never promise outcomes. No "cure", no "pain-free guaranteed", no "fix your back for good".
- She hates "state-of-the-art", "cutting-edge" and "world-class".
- US English. They always write "physical therapy", never "physiotherapy".
