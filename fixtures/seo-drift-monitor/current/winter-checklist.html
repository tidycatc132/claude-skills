Winter Roof Checklist: 9 Things to Inspect Before the First Snow

Before the first snow, walk the perimeter of your home and look for lifted shingles, cracked flashing, clogged gutters and daylight in the attic. Fixing these in October costs a fraction of an emergency repair in January.

  1. Shingles
  2. Flashing
  3. Gutters
  4. Attic ventilation
  5. Ice dam history
  6. Tree limbs
  7. Skylights
  8. Chimney cap
  9. Downspout extensions
