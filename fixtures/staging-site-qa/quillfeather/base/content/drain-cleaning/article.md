# Drain Cleaning in Portland, OR

We clear slow and blocked drains across Portland the same day.

## What causes a blocked drain?

Grease, hair and roots in older clay main lines cause most of the calls we get.

## Do you inspect the main line with a camera?

Yes. Every main line call includes a camera inspection, so you see the problem before we quote.

## What if the water heater is the real problem?

Sometimes slow hot water is not a drain issue at all; see our [water heater repair](/services/water-heater-repair/) page.
