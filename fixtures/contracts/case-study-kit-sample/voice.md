## Voice Dimensions
### Calm, not clinical
Sentences reassure before they explain. Passes: "Most cracked teeth can be saved if you call us the same day."
### Clear, not salesy
No pressure words, no countdowns. Passes: "Here is what an implant involves and what it costs to find out."
### Warm, not cute
Friendly with kids and adults, never baby talk. Passes: "Kids get a tour of the chair before anyone picks up a tool."

## Tone Matrix
| Context | Tone shift | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | most reassuring | "Scared of the dentist? So are half our new patients." |
| Blog or guide | practical | "First, rinse with warm water." |
| Email | personal | "Dr. Okafor will see you tomorrow at 9." |

## Do / Don't Examples
| Do | Don't | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "We can see you today." | "Limited slots, book now!" | no pressure |
| "Sedation helps you relax." | "Painless dentistry guaranteed." | no guarantees |
| "Implants replace a missing tooth." | "Revolutionary smile makeover." | plain words |
| "Call us first." | "Don't wait, act now." | calm |
| "Kids are welcome." | "Kiddos love us!" | warm, not cute |
