# Content Brief: How long does a tankless water heater last?

## Page Goal
Portland homeowners with an aging tank heater or a misbehaving tankless unit want one number and what decides it. The page must let them judge how long their tankless water heater will last and when descaling or replacement makes sense, then book a descaling or replacement quote. Why now: lowest KD in the cluster and an AI Overview answers it with one figure.

## Reader and Search Intent
The reader owns a home in the Portland metro, knows roughly what a tankless water heater is, and worries about a surprise failure and the replacement bill. Intent is Informational, TOFU. The SERP rewards a one-figure answer followed by a tank-versus-tankless comparison (Measured, SERP review 2026-10-16).

## Answer Target
State that a tankless water heater lasts more than 20 years against 10 to 15 years for a tank water heater (E1), and that regular descaling is what decides whether it gets there (E3).
