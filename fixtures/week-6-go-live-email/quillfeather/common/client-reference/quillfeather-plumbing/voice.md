# Quillfeather Plumbing: voice

## Voice Dimensions

### Straight, not slick
Say what happens and when. Passes: "We'll be there between 8 and 10."

### Calm, not alarmed
No scare tactics about leaks or floods. Passes: "Most slow drains are a simple clear-out."

### Friendly, not chummy
First names and plain thanks, no jokes. Passes: "Thanks, Ade. Talk soon."

## Tone Matrix

| Context | Tone shift | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | Plain and sure | "Drains cleared the same day across Portland." |
| Email | Short sentences, first names, one exclamation mark at most | "Good news, Ade. It's ready." |
| Support reply | Calm, one next step | "We'll call you back within the hour." |

## Do / Don't Examples

| Do | Don't | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Flat price before we start" | "Unbeatable prices!" | Only real promises |
| "Licensed in Oregon" | "Top-rated pros" | Use the real credential |
| "Portland, Gresham and Beaverton" | "The whole Northwest" | Be specific |

## Reading Level & Cadence

- Grade 6, average sentence 12 words, max 22, active voice, paragraphs of 1 to 3 sentences.
