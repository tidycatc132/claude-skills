# Statement of Work: Peakline Roofing website redesign

Client: Peakline Roofing LLC
Prepared: 2026-09-30

## Deliverables

1. Discovery and sitemap approval.
2. Design and build of a WordPress website of up to 8 pages. Standard utility pages (home, contact, privacy policy, terms and conditions, 404) and the blog are included at no charge and do not count toward this total.
3. On-page SEO for every page in scope.
4. Launch and 30 days of post-launch support.

## Out of scope

Copywriting beyond the 8 pages, photography, and paid media.
