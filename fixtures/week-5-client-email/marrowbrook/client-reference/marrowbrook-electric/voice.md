# Marrowbrook Electric: voice

## Voice Dimensions

### Plain, not technical
Say what the work does for the home before naming the part. Passes: "A new panel lets you run the heat pump and the dryer at the same time."

### Steady, not salesy
No urgency or hype. Passes: "We'll tell you what the house needs and what can wait."

### Neighborly, not casual
Warm and polite, never slangy. Passes: "Thanks for having us out."

## Tone Matrix

| Context | Tone shift | Example line |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | Confident and plain | "Panel upgrades done right the first time." |
| Email | Polite and brief, first names, no exclamation marks | "Thanks, Dana. We'll be there Tuesday at 8." |
| Support reply | Calm, one next step | "We'll call you by noon with a time." |

## Do / Don't Examples

| Do | Don't | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "EV charger installed and inspected" | "Supercharge your ride!" | No hype |
| "Licensed master electricians" | "Expert pros" | Use the real credential |
| "We'll tell you what can wait" | "Act now" | No urgency |
| "Older Boise homes" | "Every home everywhere" | Be specific |
| "Quote within one business day" | "Instant quotes" | Only real promises |

## Reading Level & Cadence

- Grade 7, average sentence 14 words, max 24, active voice 90 percent, paragraphs of 1 to 3 sentences, second person often.
